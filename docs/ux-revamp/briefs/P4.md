## Why
The lead reviewed five committed final screenshots (`docs/ux-revamp/final-screens/`) and found defects that axe and the overflow check cannot see. Fix them, and add an automated guard so this class of defect cannot come back.

## Defects (evidence in the named images)
1. `lib-list__mobile__light.jpg`: in "Mis clases" at 375 px the five row actions (Abrir, Renombrar, Descargar audio, Descargar texto, Borrar) are squeezed into one row, so their labels overlap each other. Root cause: the global rule `button { flex: 1; min-width: 100px; cursor: pointer }` in `app/static/css/base.css`, kept by F1a to avoid visual change. Remove that global flex/min-width rule (keep `cursor: pointer` and the disabled cursor), then FIX every layout that relied on it with explicit local rules. The actions must wrap onto two rows or become a compact, accessible arrangement (44 px targets, visible labels or accessible names), and keep working with keyboard.
2. `transcript-finished__mobile__dark.jpg` and `lib-list__mobile__light.jpg`: page and view titles (`h1`, `h2` with `tabindex="-1"` that the app focuses programmatically on view changes and at the end of a run) show a heavy yellow or black focus box. Headings are not in the tab order, so suppress the focus ring for `[tabindex="-1"]` headings only (`:focus` and `:focus-visible`), keep the 3 px ring for every real control. The screen-reader behavior (focus moves to the heading) must stay.
3. `run-progress-multi__desktop__light.jpg`: the run view ends with a heading "Lo que llevamos" that has nothing under it, followed by the transcript's own heading "Transcripción". Remove the empty duplicate heading (markup in `app/templates/index.html`, logic in `app/static/js/status/run-view.js`) and check no test or script depends on it. In `transcript-finished__mobile__dark.jpg` the search field placeholder "Buscar en la transcripción" is cut off at 375 px: shorten the visible placeholder to "Buscar" (the accessible name and label stay "Buscar en la transcripción").
4. Screenshot artifact: in full-page captures (`player-segment-playing__desktop__dark.jpg`) the docked player and the toast are drawn in the middle of the page. In `tools/ux/final_screens.mjs` force docked and fixed elements to `position: static` for full-page captures only (as the D0 notes describe), then regenerate the images of the states that are affected AND of every state whose CSS changed. Re-run the privacy scan (`tools/ux/privacy_scan.mjs`) for every regenerated image and update `INDEX.md` and `tests/test_final_screens.py` expectations if needed (still synthetic only, still under 15 MB).
